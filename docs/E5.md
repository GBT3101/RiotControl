# E5: music themes and copy for the nine new cities

E5 gives each new city its own adaptive music theme and its own copy, using the per-city
template in docs/E0.md §6. Madrid, London and Paris are unchanged.

## Files

| What | Files |
| --- | --- |
| Music | `src/audio/music/cities/{budapest,berlin,stockholm,vienna,amsterdam,rome,barcelona,prague,milan}.ts`, one line each in `cities/index.ts` |
| Copy | `src/ui/text/city/<same nine>.ts`, one line each in `city/index.ts` |
| New patches (shared) | `src/audio/music/instruments.ts`: `fiddle`, `nyckel`, `cimbalom`, `clarinet`, `organ`, `mandolin`, `clap` (additive; existing patches untouched) |
| Dev page | `src/audio/dev/main.ts`, "city themes" block: one row per city with **hold · calm · tense · climax · night** buttons, plus an offline loudness table |
| Test | `tests/audio-music.test.ts`: "produces valid bars for every style…" now has a 30 s timeout. It walks ~104k notes for 12 cities × 4 themes and ran ~5 s, past the 5 s default on a loaded machine |
| Images | `docs/progress/e5-music.png` (spectrograms), `docs/progress/e5-postcards.png`, `docs/progress/e5-papers.png` |

## Music

Each city has a `hold` (prep phase) and a `march` (waves) in the existing kit (kit.ts
vocabulary, `marchDrums` etc.). The sequencer's adaptive layers still apply:

| Layer | Adds |
| --- | --- |
| L1 (calm) | melody, bass, light drums |
| L2 | chord stabs, full snare cadence |
| L3 (tense) | harmony line, glockenspiel, crashes |
| L4 (climax) | the Ministry's chiptune octave lead, crashes every bar |

Each city's drum function adds its own layer behaviour on top of these. The Ministry-march
character stays in every theme: snare rolls into section ends, the oom-pah bass, the chip lead
at L4 and the minor night variant. Every A-section march tune, and some holds, are hand-written
original melodies in folk idioms. B sections come from the seeded generator, using
city-specific rhythm vocabularies and chord-scales.

| City | Hold (prep) | March (waves) and how it escalates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budapest | Csárdás **lassú**, D Hungarian minor (augmented second F–G♯ over the tonic), 66–70 bpm, fiddle over cimbalom tremolo | **Friss** 2/4, fiddle over cimbalom "esz-tam" offbeats. Its tempo range of 120 → 166 bpm is the widest of any city, so it accelerates as the crowd grows. Boot-slap claps from L3 |
| Vienna | Viennese waltz in D, ~170 bpm, fiddles; the horn "pah" lands early on beat 2 (step 3.5 of 12) | Pompous 2/4 Austrian march in E♭ with trumpets and glockenspiel. New Year's-concert clap-along on every beat from L3 and on the off-beats at L4 |
| Prague | Bohemian *valčík* (waltz) in F, clarinet with horns | Polka in B♭: clarinet lead, tuba oom-pah, brass "pahs", a trumpet a third below from L3 |
| Berlin | Weimar-cabaret foxtrot in A minor: clarinet, bar-room e-piano on 2 and 4, brushes | Prussian march in F (brass, dotted fanfares). **L3:** a four-on-the-floor kick, off-beat hats, a backbeat clap and an off-beat tuba pedal under the march. **L4:** the snare cadence drops out for 16th hats and an acid chip riff; only the march tune and the section-end snare rolls are left of Prussia |
| Stockholm | 16th-note **polska** in D minor (3/4, stamp on 1 and 3), nyckelharpa over an open D–A drone | **Gånglåt** (folk walking march) in D minor, 2/4, nyckelharpa + drone, fiddle answering from L3. The `nyckel` patch carries its own sympathetic ring and a cold 230 ms stone-hall echo |
| Amsterdam | Draaiorgel (street-organ) waltz in G | Draaiorgel march in G: organ lead, counter-melody and chords, woodblock ticks from L2, the organ's glockenspiel register from L3 |
| Rome | Serenade in F: mandolin tremolo over strummed guitar | **Tarantella** in A minor, 6/8 (`stepsPerBeat: 3`), mandolin over tambourine. From L3 the brass doubles the tune a third below (the "opera chorus"), and every section ends on a **grand brass swell** of the tonic chord with a timpani roll |
| Barcelona | **Havanera** (Catalan sailors' song), D minor verse → D major refrain, horn singing triplets over the dotted bass | **Rumba catalana** in E minor, 4/4: palmas and palmas sordas from L1, the guitar ventilador strum (pluck on 0 3 4 6 8 11 12 14) and golpe from L2, trumpet lead |
| Milan | Opera aria in F (3/4), clarinet over harp-like guitar | Verdi-manner opera march in B♭ (original tune), the fastest march (128 → 152 bpm). Trumpet lead, clarinets on the banda "pahs", bass drum + piatti on every downbeat from L2, timpani at L3. The B section turns to B♭ minor |

Fanfares (the victory lead): fiddle, trumpet, clarinet, brass, nyckel, organ, brass, trumpet,
trumpet.

Copyright: every melody is newly written in its idiom. None quotes a real tune (no Radetzky,
no Blue Danube, no Funiculì, no Va pensiero).

### New patches (instruments.ts)

| Patch | Synthesis | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fiddle` | saw through a wooden body (190 Hz low cut, +7 dB at 2.8 kHz), bow attack with rosin noise, late vibrato | Budapest, Vienna, Stockholm |
| `nyckel` | buzzier bowed tone (saw + square), sympathetic partials ringing on, darker echo 230 ms later | Stockholm |
| `cimbalom` | two detuned strings per strike, felt-hammer filter sweep, metal tick; notes ≥ 0.6 s become a 13 Hz tremolo (≤ 12 strikes) | Budapest |
| `clarinet` | square through a woody low-pass, breath on the attack | Prague, Berlin, Milan |
| `organ` | flue triangle + reed saw at +11 cents (celeste), a quiet 4′ square, 6.3 Hz bellows tremulant, wind noise | Amsterdam |
| `mandolin` | doubled steel course; notes ≥ 0.22 s are tremolo-picked at 14 Hz | Rome |
| `clap` | three smeared slaps + room tail (percussion) | Budapest, Vienna, Berlin, Barcelona |

Single-note levels (A4, 0.5 s, vel 0.85) were matched to the existing leads: trumpet −21,
brass −24, accordion −26, pluck −30 dB RMS. New patches: fiddle −26, nyckel −25, clarinet −26,
organ −26, cimbalom −32 and mandolin −31 (both decaying plucks, like pluck), clap peak −13
(snare −8.5).

Every hold falls in the existing −23.5 … −28 band and every march in −21.6 … −24.4 (the existing
three: −22.1 … −24.4). Peaks stay at −6.9 dBFS or below, with no clipping. Rebalancing on the
way: the Amsterdam organ bass gave way to the tuba bass pipes (it sat 4 dB low). Berlin's techno
layers were 2 dB hot; they no longer double the march kicks and the pedal and acid riff were
lowered. The Barcelona havanera bass is shorter and its kick softer.

**Listen proxy** (`docs/progress/e5-music.png`: log-frequency spectrograms 60 Hz–9 kHz plus peak
envelopes, rows = cities, columns = hold / calm / tense / climax). Each structure shows in the
plots:

- Budapest lassú: half-note cimbalom blocks with tremolo striations under long fiddle lines.
- Stockholm: continuous horizontal drone bands.
- Berlin calm vs. climax: a sparse march becomes a dense low pulse with constant hats.
- Rome: the dotted tarantella grid with the swell at section ends.
- Barcelona: the regular strum lattice.

From calm to climax the spectral centroid rises in every theme except Berlin (Budapest
258 → 357 Hz, Milan 533 → 627 Hz, Rome 213 → 318 Hz). Berlin's centroid stays flat
(337 → 328 Hz) because the techno kick and pedal add as much low end as the hats add top: its
200 Hz–1 kHz share rises from 17.8 % to 20.3 %, and its loudness from −23.2 to −21.6 dB.

How to re-measure:

- **Dev page:** `npm run dev` → `/audio.html` → "city themes": audition buttons per city and
  layer, and **measure loudness offline** for the RMS/peak table.
- **Headless:** call `window.__audioDev.renderMusic({ phase, city, level, crowd, night, seconds })`,
  as `src/audio/dev/check.mjs` does.

## Copy

Each city's copy has: tagline, Capitol name, 4 victory + 3 defeat decks, the Minister's welcome
(Budapest's opens the tutorial), 4 flavour quips, a masthead (title · dateline · motto) and 7–8
sign slogans. Each satirises power and bureaucracy on both sides and never targets the people.
There are no real politicians, ethnic or religious targets, or tragedies.

### Glyph coverage

All copy was checked against each font it is drawn in: small/mono for the postcard, decks and
advisor; large for the masthead title; the 3×5 sign font for slogans.

- **Missing everywhere:** `ő ű` (Hungarian), `å` (Swedish), `ß` (German), and every háček
  letter `č ř š ž ě ů ý` (Czech). The copy avoids them: no "Skål", "Grüß Gott" or "Pražský",
  and the Capitol is "Prague Castle" rather than "Pražský hrad".
- **Sign font:** accents collapse to base letters (É È Ê Á À Ó Í Ú Ñ Ç), so the slogans only
  use those plus A–Z.
- **Slogan width:** every slogan is ≤ 21 px, the existing maximum (MERDE). SERVUS, GEZEUR,
  OSTRES, FRETTA and NA UND (23 px) were swapped for narrower words.
- **Phone mastheads:** on a phone-portrait page (≈183 px wide), the first choices "Der
  Ordnungsbote", "Der Amtsschimmel" and "Corriere dell'Ordine" overflowed, and two datelines ran
  off the edge. They became "Der Ordner" (it means both a file folder and a steward), "Amtsschimmel"
  and "Il Puntuale". Every title now fits with its crest seals, or title-only as London's does.
  Long mottos drop on narrow pages the same way Madrid's and Paris's do.

### Budapest: the tutorial city

The tutorial's only city-specific pieces are the welcome line and `{choke}`, the blueprint
chokepoint nearest the Capitol's front steps. For Budapest that resolves to **Alkotmány
utca**:

- "Üdvözöljük! Budapest: two cities, one river, one Ministry. Your first posting."
- then "I am your Minister of the Interior…"
- then "More cops on **Alkotmány utca**, close together. Lone cops get rammed." This is 3 lines on
  desktop and 3 on phone.

When there is no chokepoint, the fallback name is `capitol: 'Országház'`. Nothing in
src/ui/tutorial needs changing; wiring the tutorial to Budapest is E6's job.

The same check across all cities found one long chokepoint name: Amsterdam's "Damstraat
(Oudezijds Voorburgwal)" makes the line 4 lines on desktop (bubble limit 3). It only matters if
the tutorial runs there (E6 plans to skip it outside Budapest). The fix belongs to E2: a shorter
chokepoint name.

### Fields the format does not have

`CityCopy` has no per-district or per-street flavour lines and no per-city headlines or
closers: those are global. The Europe card's one-line `note` belongs to E1 (src/ui/campaign.ts).
