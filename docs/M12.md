# M12 — Balance & director tuning

Goal (PLAN §1.5): waves grow from dozens (wave 1 = 30) to thousands, a run to **5000
Legitimacy takes 35–50 min at 1×** for a competent player, a passive player loses, a decent one
wins, and level-ups arrive in time to answer the protester types they unlock. Owner-fixed numbers
(costs, Legitimacy on death, level thresholds, Hate payouts, start 100 Hate / 0 Legit, win 5000,
unlock levels) are untouched and test-pinned (`tests/balance-curves.test.ts`).

Doing nothing (`none`) loses in wave 4 (~4 min). Mobile tier (`--quality mobile`, 2 seeds, balanced /
escalate / cheap / passive): the crowd peaks exactly at the 1500 cap and outcomes match
desktop (balanced 33–38 min, escalate 45–62 min, cheap 28–35 min, passive loses at waves 9–11).
Sim cost: 0.2–0.5 ms/tick average; a 3 × 5 × 3 sweep takes ~3.5 min on 4 cores.

A balanced run, Madrid seed 2 (36.9 min): 12.6k protesters fallen (3311 students … 577
Prophets), 476 officers lost (310 riot, 52 armed, 24 sniper, 17 soldier, 15 brigade members,
11 humvees, 1 tank), 17.4k Hate earned / 15.7k spent. Hate held: ~100 for the first 10 min,
300–1000 mid game, 1500–2500 late (tank/heli money a few times, not spam). Legitimacy every
5 min: 0 · 10 · 35 · 280 · 750 · 1265 · 2330 · 4025.

**Reading the table.** The bots deploy at most ~1 unit/s (token bucket, bursts of 3), never
misclick and react instantly, so treat their times as the fast end for that play style; a
competent human should land around 38–48 min. All five strategies stay inside one band:

- **balanced** 33–39 min with real Capitol tension (lowest integrity 59–94%).
- **escalate** (saving for the newest toy every time) 42–55 min with the biggest hordes,
  2300–2450 alive at once.
- **cheap** (only ≤ 10-Hate units) wins about as fast as balanced. It is borderline in London,
  where the Capitol falls as low as 36%, and its middle game stalls: L4 comes at 16–22 min
  because gas and rubber kill everything before anything dies.
- **sacrificial** (feeding officers up the approaches) is viable and about as fast (31–36 min),
  but not faster than cheap and not much faster than balanced.
- **passive** loses at waves 9–11.

The new `ProtesterDef.resist` field gives each protester type a fraction of incoming damage it
ignores, per damage type. Late-tier protesters shrug off non-lethal tools, so the Ministry has to
escalate to real weapons: the escalation spiral as a mechanic.

### Sim (`src/sim/*`)

- **Director**: waves stream in over `spawnSeconds(size)`, and newly unlocked types ramp in via
  the director's `joined` map. Waves are still generated from (wave index, level, elapsed time).
- **Placement**: ground units (not blockades) may stand on the **Capitol steps**. Before this,
  protesters attacking London's 12-tile-wide steps were out of reach of every L0 unit, so a
  careful player still lost. The ghost overlay already uses `canDeploy`, so the steps glow
  automatically.
- **Gas clouds** stun on entry only. They used to re-stun every pulse, which froze everything in
  a 2.5-tile radius until it died. The gas DoT and slow are unchanged.
- **Combat**: `hurtProtester` applies the per-type resistance table (`World.resist`, built from
  data at construction).
- **Bots** (`src/sim/bots.ts`, re-exported from `headless.ts` with the same `new Bot(world, kind)`
  API): `none · passive · riot (legacy) · cheap · balanced · escalate · sacrificial`.
  - `MapIntel` traces every spawn door down the flow field to estimate traffic per road tile,
    plus road widths and rooftop coverage.
  - Shared rules: a deploy token bucket (`BOT_TUNING`), a last line on the steps placed where
    attackers stand, guards for rooftop units, charged gas grenades thrown on cooldown,
    commandable units sent to the densest crowd, and calling waves early when safe.
  - When Legitimacy stalls for 150 s, balanced and escalate push one riot officer up an
    approach, as a player who has learned the rules would.
- **Headless runner**: `runHeadless()` now returns a `trace` (level times, wave starts/sizes, min
  integrity, Hate/crowd samples every 10 s, Legitimacy per minute).
- `src/sim/playtest.ts`: `runPlaytest(job)` (JSON summary) and `applyOverrides('a.b=1,…')`, which
  patches `BALANCE`, `units.<id>…`, `protesters.<id>…` or `bot.…` in place for parameter
  sweeps. This is tooling only; the game never calls it.

## Rationale (what the sweeps taught us)

- **Legitimacy comes only from your own dead officers**, so the run's pace is set by attrition,
  not by kills. Cheap units are the most Legitimacy-efficient, and they even earn Hate when they
  die: Riot Control costs 5 and pays +10 Hate +5 Legit. With the owner-fixed economy, sacrifice
  is always fast (the satire). Tuning therefore targets the channels each strategy farms:
  - thrown-off snipers: climbers per roof, climb time;
  - smashed blockades: blockade HP;
  - dead gas shooters: gas shooter HP.
- **Win time barely moves with protester lethality.** Weaker weapons let the player field more
  units, which then die anyway. It does move with wave cadence: breathers, the spawn window and
  the growth rate.
- **The level multiplier is positive feedback** (losses → levels → bigger, deadlier waves). It is
  now small (3%/level). Escalation comes from the new protester types, which ramp in instead of
  flooding.
- **Gas was the dominant weapon.** Stun-locking clouds plus a 1 s cone stun every 0.25 s won
  every fight at L3 and stalled escalating players there (nothing died, so no Legitimacy).
- **A 5000 HP Capitol** fell to the first leak. At 18000 a careless player still loses by wave
  ~10, and a competent player dips to 60–90% in the noisy early game (London's wide steps are
  the hardest).

## How to rerun

```sh
npm run playtest                                         # 3 seeds × balanced/escalate/cheap/sacrificial/passive × 3 cities
npm run playtest -- --bots balanced --cities london --seeds 1,2,3 --verbose   # + wave sizes & legit/min
npm run playtest -- --quality mobile --seeds 2 --json shots/playtest-mobile.json
npm run playtest -- --set waves.growth=1.12,units.blockade.hp=800,bot.deployRate=0.5   # what-if sweep
npx vitest run tests/balance- tests/sim-                 # regression tests (~15 s)
```

Flags: `--bots`, `--cities`, `--seeds N|a,b,c`, `--minutes` (cap, default 65), `--quality`, `--jobs`
(default min(4, cores)), `--json file`, `--verbose`, `--set path=value,…`. Each worker loads the TS
sim once through Vite's module runner; runs use sim time only.

Regression tests:

- `tests/balance-curves.test.ts` (fast): owner-fixed numbers, wave curve shape, spawn windows,
  breathers, intro ramp.
- `tests/balance-scenarios.test.ts` (~13 s, real maps):
  - no defence loses by wave 6, and passive loses in waves 6–15;
  - balanced holds the Capitol above 40% for 15 min, with L1 before 5 min and L4–L8 by 15 min;
  - bots are deterministic;
  - a forced late wave exceeds 1500 on desktop and respects the mobile cap.

## For the UI / tutorial (M9/M10)

- **Tell the player how Legitimacy works early**, because it is counter-intuitive: progress comes
  from losing officers, not from killing protesters. The bots stall without a "feed a riot cop
  forward" rule, and human players will too. Good advisor lines: "Legitimacy stalled? The
  Ministry needs martyrs" and "An officer on the front line is worth five in reserve".
- **Recommended opening**:
  - Riot Control on the Capitol steps first. The steps are now deployable, and the ghost glows
    there.
  - Then a line on the busiest approach.
  - At L1, snipers on roofs **next to** riot officers. A guarded roof cannot be climbed, while an
    unguarded sniper gets thrown off (worth 10 Legit, which is the joke).
  - At L2, blockades across narrow streets.
  - At L3, gas behind the riot line, throwing grenades into dense crowds (G).
- **Escalation hint** at L6–L8: "Rubber and gas barely tickle the cultists — approve real
  weapons." Late protester types resist non-lethal damage (cultists 50% vs rubber, Prophets 60%
  vs rubber and gas).
- **Calling waves early** is only +8–12 Hate. Frame it as a tempo choice, not an economy one.
- **Late game**: Hate piles up to 1.5–2.5k (tank/heli money). The helicopter can't die, so it
  grants no Legitimacy. Tell players it protects the Capitol but doesn't advance the win.
- A new protester type arrives as a trickle for ~3 waves, which is a good moment for the
  "NEW THREAT" card.

## Owner flags / known issues

- PLAN §1.3/§1.4 tables still show the M6 baselines. The tuned stats above should be copied in
  (orchestrator). Values not in the brief:
  - breather 16–24 s (PLAN 12–20);
  - protester resistances;
  - Capitol 18000 HP.
- Cheap-unit spam is the quickest route in London (28–33 min) and is safe in Madrid. It is
  inherent to the owner-fixed Legitimacy-per-Hate ratio; the counter-pressure is the Capitol
  and the late resistant types.
- The escalate bot hoards Hate (up to ~24k in Madrid) because it only ever buys the newest unit
  and caps helicopters at 3. That is the bot's behaviour, not the economy; balanced peaks at
  ~2–13k.
- Bot decisions take up to ~20 ms each, once per simulated second. That's fine headless; the
  `?autoplay` attract mode (escalate bot) may show a small hitch once per second, so throttle or
  cap `MapIntel` scans if it shows in M13.
- Max tick times of 200–450 ms seen in the parallel sweeps are worker contention and GC. A lone
  40-min run has no step above 30 ms.
