# E1 — Campaign map of Europe

**PLAY** now opens the Ministry's war-room map, a worn map sheet taped to an oak desk. All
twelve campaign cities have a push pin. Tapping a pin (or its name tag) opens that city's
dossier card. A built city gets the postcard with its records and **DEPLOY**, which starts
the run exactly as the old city select did. A city whose map isn't built yet gets a
construction file (blueprint photo, hazard tape, its note) stamped **UNDER CONSTRUCTION**.
Budapest is marked **LEVEL 1 · TUTORIAL**, on its tag and on its card, and is selected (with a
pulsing ring) on the first visit.

## How it works

- **Data-driven pins.** A pin is red with DEPLOY on its card when `isPlayable(id)`, otherwise
  it is grey with hazard tape on its tag. A city lights up as soon as its blueprint line lands
  in `src/maps/cities/index.ts`. The pin's position comes from `CAMPAIGN` lat/lon through the
  same projection as the map.
- **Borders.** Each land border is one arc shared by both neighbours, so they meet exactly.
  The assembler orients each piece. Big, mostly off-map countries (Russia, Kazakhstan, Iran,
  …) are `underlay`s drawn first. The tests check that every ring closes, that no arc is used
  by more than two countries, and that each city lies inside its own country.
- **Colour.** DSatur with a hard constraint (land neighbours never share a family). Soft
  costs keep look-alike families (cream/peach/tan…) and countries close across a strait
  apart, spread usage by count and painted area, and keep the loud lilac for small countries.
- **Zoom.** Zoom is in device px per map px and is an integer at rest. While the user
  pinches or the wheel eases, it is fractional; it settles on the nearest integer.
  - min: the whole sheet fits.
  - default: the closest zoom that frames the 12 pins plus tag room, capped at 1 map px per
    UI px.
  - max: default + 3.
  - Defaults by screen: 1440×900 → 2 (whole map); 1920×1080 → 3; phones → 3.
- **Tags.** Tags are UI-scale. They are laid out per integer zoom: the selected city first,
  then Level 1, then the most crowded pins. A tag never covers a pin, another tag, a thread,
  or the printed compass and scale bar. Spots on screen beside the card are tried first. A
  long crimson thread is used only when the near spots are taken. Country and sea labels under
  a tag or pin are hidden at that zoom. After a drag the tags re-flow to the part of the map
  now on screen, and the camera pans once so the selected tag is fully visible.
- **Touch.** The tap target is within 22 CSS px of a pin head (44 px targets, nearest pin
  wins) or anywhere on a visible tag. Pinch uses the screen's own pointer tracking on the
  host. Single-finger drag and wheel go through the UI input router.
- **Reduced motion.** With the "screen shake" setting off or `prefers-reduced-motion`, the
  pin bob, the pulse and the sea shimmer are static.
- **Performance.** The sheet, waves, labels and desk are built once and cached with
  `uiTex`. The build costs about 0.5 s on the main thread; `prewarmEuropeMap()` runs it in
  idle slices while the title shows. The attract game under the desk is paused and hidden
  while the map is up.

- Through `window.__riot.ui.topScreen`: `select('madrid')`, `zoomStep(±1, -1, -1)`.
- Keys: Esc returns to the title, Enter deploys, Tab cycles cities, + and − zoom, arrows pan.
