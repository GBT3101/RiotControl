# E4 North — Capitols + landmarks: Berlin, Stockholm, Amsterdam

Source: `src/art/landmarks/{berlin,stockholm,amsterdam}/` (one `LandmarkCity` each, listed in
`src/art/landmarks/cities.ts`). Same engine, conventions, overlays and damage model as M3b
(`docs/art/M3b.md`): analytic iso ray-caster + hand-written materials, RIOT-64 only, sun from the
upper left, coloured exterior outline, anchor = footprint top vertex, the sprite paints its own
ground, front (+v / SW) face is the lit main face.

Previews (`docs/progress/`):

- `e4-north-capitols.png` (2×): Congreso · Westminster · Bourbon (top) beside Reichstag ·
  Riksdagshuset · Paleis op de Dam (bottom), same scale.
- `e4-north-damage.png` (1×): states 0 → 4 for the three Capitols.
- `e4-north-landmarks.png` (2×): the ten secondary landmarks with their ground shadows.

Review images can also be composed by the M3b test (`SPECS` now lists the three cities):
`M3B_EXPORT=1 npx vitest run tests/landmarks-preview.test.ts` (writes `m3b-<city>.png`).

## Known gaps / ideas

- The Quadriga, Goldelse and the cog weathervane are small (≈26 / 11 / 10 px wide); they read as
  silhouettes, not sculpture.
- National Monument reliefs are suggested by light / shade bands only.
- Only state 0 has a night mask (as in M3b).
- Riksdagshuset's bowed front is a stylisation of the Norrbro façade (the real east front is a
  long curved neo-baroque range with the central columned bay).
